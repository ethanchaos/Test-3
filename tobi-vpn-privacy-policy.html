Depending on the features you use, TOBI VPN may collect or process the following information:

  • Username or account ID.
  • Account authentication information.
  • Account status and access permissions.
  • Subscription, expiry and service entitlement information.

C. VPN Connection and Usage Information

When you connect to TOBI VPN, our service processes information required to establish and maintain your VPN connection. This may include:

  • Selected VPN server or network.
  • Connection status and session identifiers.
  • Connection start and end information.
  • Technical connection information, including IP addresses received by our service infrastructure.
  • Data-transfer totals, including uploaded and downloaded data, where usage measurement is enabled.

We use relevant connection and usage information to operate the VPN service, manage customer accounts, enforce applicable usage limits, maintain security and troubleshoot technical problems.

The VPN service must process your internet traffic to route it through the selected VPN server. We do not use the contents of VPN traffic for advertising.

When advertising services are active, Google and its advertising technology may process information such as:

  • IP address.

  • Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

Google processes information under its own privacy policies and applicable advertising settings. Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

E. Support and Communications

If you contact us for assistance, we may process the information you provide, such as your account details, contact information and the contents of your support request.

We use collected information for purposes including:

  • Creating, authenticating and managing customer accounts.
  • Establishing and maintaining VPN connections.
  • Providing access to assigned VPN networks and servers.
  • Managing subscriptions, account status and device limits.
  • Measuring data usage where usage measurement is enabled.
  • Maintaining service security and preventing unauthorized access.
  • Detecting abuse, fraud and violations of service rules.
  • Displaying and measuring advertisements.
  • Providing customer support.
  • Maintaining, troubleshooting and improving the application and service.

4. VPN Traffic and Privacy

TOBI VPN uses VPN technology to route your internet traffic through a selected VPN server.

When connected, the VPN tunnel encrypts traffic between your device and the VPN endpoint using the configured VPN protocol.

Our service may process technical connection information and data-transfer totals necessary to operate the service. The contents of your internet communications are not used by us for advertising.

Please remember that a VPN does not make you completely anonymous. Websites, applications and other services you access may independently collect information about you.
