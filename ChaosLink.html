Everything you need to share a connection

Simple on the surface. Full control underneath.

Two ways to host

Switch between Hotspot and Wi-Fi Direct with one tap. Wi-Fi Direct keeps its own network name and password, and supports push-button WPS joining.

Know your devices

See every phone that has joined, which are online, and the brand, model and system of each one.

Block apps

Stop chosen apps from using the connection on connected phones.

Data and usage

Track download and upload, set limits, check history and run a speed test.

Privacy and filtering

Block sites and ads and choose your DNS for everyone on your link.

Auto-detect proxy

Pick HTTP, SOCKS or both. The port is yours to set, 8080 by default.

Join someone else’s host

Open the Link screen on any phone, let Chaos Link find a host on your Wi-Fi automatically, and tap Connect. You see live download, upload, speed and time.

Always on

Keep screen on and Keep group alive recreate your Wi-Fi Direct group if it drops.

Your profile

Set a photo, username, device name and avatar colour. Your photo stays on your phone.

Online in three steps

  1. Choose a modePick Hotspot or Wi-Fi Direct on the home screen.
  2. Tap StartYour network name, password, port and IP appear. Share them with the QR code.
  3. Connect other phonesOn the other phone, open Link, join the host’s Wi-Fi, then tap Connect.

How do I install it?

Open the downloaded file. If Android blocks it, go to Settings, allow your browser to install unknown apps, then open the file again.

Do both phones need the app?

The host needs Chaos Link. Phones joining through the Link screen need it too.

Does it use my mobile data?

Yes. Phones on your host use your phone’s connection, so use Data and usage to set limits.
